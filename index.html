About

I've been doing this for twenty years, in one form or another — bars, bands, other people's living rooms. I started writing songs young, mostly to get through losing people, then later to get through loving them. Going solo was the scariest thing I've done in a while, and also the most obvious. I book my own shows, run my own socials, and I'm finally making the record I've wanted to make the whole time.
