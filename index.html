About

Twenty years in, running it herself

Christina's spent the last two decades playing bars, breweries, private events, and festivals across the Carolinas.  When she's solo she brings one guitar and a catalog deep enough to carry any room, and any crowd. Along the way, she started writing her own songs too, and that's turned into a five-song EP, led by the single "Twisted Crazy Beautiful."
